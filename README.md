The Digital Ration Distribution System is a web-based application developed to modernize and digitize the traditional ration distribution process under the Public Distribution System (PDS). 
In India, the PDS plays a significant role in providing essential food grains and commodities such as rice, wheat, sugar, and kerosene to economically weaker sections of society at subsidized prices. 
The system is regulated by the Government of India under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution.
Despite its importance, the traditional ration distribution system faces several problems such as corruption, fake ration cards, stock mismanagement, lack of transparency, and manual record maintenance. 
These issues reduce the efficiency and reliability of the system and affect the beneficiaries who depend on it.
The Digital Ration Distribution System aims to solve these problems by introducing an online platform where all activities such as user registration, stock management, notification system, OTP verification, and ration distribution are digitally managed. 
The system ensures transparency, accountability, and fairness in the distribution process.
In this system, beneficiaries will receive notifications when stock is available. They will be given a time slot to collect ration, reducing long queues. At the time of distribution, OTP authentication will be used to verify the beneficiary’s identity, ensuring that ration is distributed only to the rightful person.
This project is developed using HTML and CSS for frontend design, PHP for backend processing, and MySQL for database management
The application connects administrators, fair-price shopkeepers, and citizens on a 
single digital platform. By automating the verification and distribution process, 
the system ensures that essential commodities reach eligible families in a fair, 
transparent, and time-efficient manner.
